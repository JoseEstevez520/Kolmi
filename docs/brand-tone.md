# Brand tone

## What it is

The class's collaborative space, where we learn together. It starts with notes that the AI
turns into shared notes, and will grow with more ways to contribute: a chat with the notes,
forums and whatever the class needs.

## Core idea

Learn as a hive. Everyone adds a little and together something whole comes out, whether it's
a note, a doubt or an answer.

**Slogan:** Learn as a hive.

## Principle

Useful first, hive second. Every message gives the clear information, and the hive shows up
as a touch that brings it to life, never as something you have to decode.

## How Kolmi talks

- **Clear first**: the user understands what happened without thinking.
- **Close**: uses "you", short sentences.
- **In plural**: "the hive", "together", "the class".
- **Hive vocabulary, in moderation**: hive, contribute, work, buzz, honey… One per message
  at most.
- An occasional 🐝 or 🍯 emoji in confirmations; in errors and important information, never.

## Examples in the app

| Situation | Text |
|---|---|
| Welcome | "Welcome to the hive. Here we learn together." |
| Note sent | "Note sent 🐝 Tonight the hive adds it to the notes." |
| Notes updated | "The hive has been busy: 3 topics updated with your notes." |
| Empty | "The hive is quiet. Be the first to contribute." |
| Your contributions | "You've contributed to the hive 12 times." |
| Error | "Couldn't send it. Try again." |

## Where how much hive goes

- **App**: a touch in welcomes, confirmations and empty states. Buttons, menus and errors,
  neutral.
- **Landing and README**: hive all the way, with contributions as drops, notes as honeycomb,
  "the hive works at night" and the slogan.
- **New features (chat, forums…)**: same rule, clear name first and a hive touch after. For
  example, "Chat · Ask the hive".
